About Health360

Health360 is a digital wellness project designed to help people organize everyday health information and build awareness of their personal wellness habits.

Our Mission

Make everyday wellness tracking simpler by bringing useful information into one convenient place.

Instead of keeping separate notes for daily activities, users can use one platform to organize their wellness records.

What We Offer

  • One place for wellness information
  • Daily activity and hydration records
  • Sleep tracking records
  • A personal wellness journal
  • An easy-to-understand dashboard
