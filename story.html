About Health360

Health360 is a digital wellness project designed to help users organize their everyday wellness information in one convenient place.

The Problem

People may keep information about physical activity, sleep, hydration and other wellness habits in different places. This can make it difficult to review their overall daily routine.

Our Solution

Health360 aims to bring wellness information together in an easy-to-understand interface. Users can review records, explore patterns and organize their wellness journey.

Key Features

  • Personal wellness dashboard
  • Activity and step records
  • Sleep and hydration tracking
  • Personal wellness journal
  • Visual presentation of wellness information
